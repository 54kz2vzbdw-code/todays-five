# Changelog

The full history, for the record. What people see is `whatsnew.json` (1.0 and later, a headline and up to three
items per version, rendered on the About page and in the what's-new toast); the pre-release entries below never
render. No dates anywhere, by design: versions are the marketing version plus a build number (About shows
`1.3 (build N)`), and what shipped as 4.0.0 is 1.0.

## 1.3 — A better first minute.

- **New** — The welcome is a list you can try before you keep it.
- **Improved** — Links say what they do, and saving yours fits your device.
- **New** — Shake, press S, or tap ↻ to shuffle in one-thing mode.

For the record: a texted link now shows a card (Open Graph and Twitter tags, `icons/og.png`); the welcome became a
live, local list with Keep this list, Skip and Paste; the save-your-link sheet leads with Add to Home Screen on a
phone in Safari, says the icon holds the link in the installed app, and leads with Bookmark on a desktop, and ⋯
carries a Save your link row until the link is copied or confirmed (devices from before are grandfathered); the
Share sheet puts the View link first and by default, the Private link (the edit link's new name) last under a
warning, New keys (Rotate's new name) beside it, and Tell a friend at the bottom with a note about the app and the
bare app URL; shuffle in one-thing mode by a shake, S, or ↻ beside the count. URL formats are unchanged.

## 1.2 — Day and night, your way.

- **New** — Pick a Day theme and a Night theme; the sun and moon flip them.
- **New** — Every theme has a partner one tap away. Your own can make theirs.
- **Improved** — Shorter update notes, like this one.

For the record: two slots (Day, Night) and one switch (by hand, with the system, on a schedule) replace the
separate Follow system and Schedule toggles; existing settings migrate on read. Two curated kits were added so that
every kit has a designed partner (Blush for Pink, Teletype for Terminal). `T` flips, `Shift+T` opens Appearance,
⋯ → Theme opens Appearance. The changelog moved to this shape.

## 1.1 — It got quieter.

- **Improved** — A line shows its menu on hover or hold.
- **Improved** — Fewer controls up top, and they fade while you work.
- **New** — Your own theme can carry a sound.

For the record: a line is the checkbox and the words; ⋯ appears on hover on the desktop (click for the menu, drag
to move), a hold lifts a line on the phone (drag to move, let go for the menu), swipe right opens the menu, swipe
left is Not today. The rail is the date, the count with the sync dot, the two views, Share and ⋯; Theme, Sound and
Full screen moved into ⋯ (T, M and F still work); on the desktop the rail and footer fade when the mouse rests. The
tour was retired: the first list teaches the basics and three one-line hints show once each. A theme you make can
carry a sound pack (`T2:` codes; `T1:` codes still import). The version scheme became a marketing version plus a
build number, and what shipped as 4.0.0 was renumbered 1.0.

## 1.0 — The first full release.

- **New** — Repeating lines, Not today, one-thing mode, search, templates, add from anywhere.
- **New** — One Settings sheet, and delete a list everywhere with ten seconds to undo.
- **Fixed** — Sound on the iPhone after a call.

For the record (it shipped as 4.0.0): lines that repeat (every day, weekdays, chosen days, monthly) and come back
undone on their next day; "Not today" (swipe left, `-`, or the line menu); one-thing mode (`O` or the count);
search in Everything (`/`); templates saved from a section; a line's menu can move it to another list; Recently
deleted with Restore; who's here (a dot per other open device); a day review card; a theme schedule; three new
sound packs (typewriter, marble, pop); export as JSON or Markdown and import; an add-from-anywhere URL for
Shortcuts and bookmarklets; "Delete this list everywhere" with a ten-second undo; a what's-new toast after an
update. One Settings sheet (⋯ → Settings) holds every preference. Sound sticks on the iPhone after a call or a
lock.

## 0.3 (pre-release; shipped as v3)

- Every list is encrypted on your device before it goes anywhere. The link is the key; a view link lets someone
  watch without touching.
- Fonts and the realtime client are served from this site—nothing loads from Google or a CDN—and the phone menu
  became a proper bottom sheet.
- A five-step tour on the first list, and a save-your-link sheet you can't miss.

## 0.2 (pre-release; shipped as v2)

- Sync between devices through a secret link, no account. Sections and several lists; Everything holds the
  backlog.
- Twelve curated themes plus your own from one accent colour, each with its own fonts, sounds and confetti.
  Installable on the phone and the Mac.

## 0.1 (pre-release; shipped as v1)

- Five lines in big type, a strike that draws across, a knock or a bell, confetti, and a finale when the list is
  done.
