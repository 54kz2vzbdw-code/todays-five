# Today's Five 1.7 audit — Accessibility: tooling

(The agent could not write this file itself; the text below is its final report, verbatim, saved by the orchestrator. Scripts, JSON data and screenshots referenced are in this directory.)

Lens: automated measurement (contrast math, target-size geometry, keyboard/focus traces, animation/live-region instrumentation). Source read at build 76; app served locally at `http://127.0.0.1:8796/?transport=local`.

## Findings

### Theme engine under-guarantees accentText/accent/danger contrast on elevated surfaces (curated kits only)
- severity: bug
- environment: pure Node (theme.js math) + desktop 1440×900, longtime fixture, browser-confirmed
- steps: 1. `node contrast-node.mjs` — imports theme.js, checks all 12 curated kits + 20 seeded custom themes against the token pairs styles.css actually draws with (text/muted/dim/done/accentText/danger/hairSolid/accent-as-focus-ring, against ink/ink-2/ink-3). 2. Apply Harbor as the night theme (`localStorage tf/v2/meta → device.night = "T1:curated:harbor"`, `device.slot = "night"`, `device.switch = {mode:"hand",...}` — deleting `switch` instead re-triggers `migrateSlots()`, which re-derives night from the stale legacy `dev.theme` field and silently discards the edit), reload, read `getComputedStyle` on the real elements.
- evidence: `contrast-node.mjs`, `contrast-results.json` (748 checks), `contrast-fails.json` (18 failing, all 18 in curated kits, 0 in the 20 random custom themes), `contrast-browser.mjs`, `contrast-browser-confirm.json`, `confirm-harbor-desktop-*.png`, `confirm-light-desktop-*.png`.
- why it matters: `derive()` (the code path behind every custom/"made your own" theme) checks `accentText` against `--ink-3` directly (theme.js:317,331) and checks `accent`/`danger` too, which is why all 20 random custom themes I tested passed every check with zero failures. But `finalize()` (the code path for the 9 non-original curated kits) fixes `accentText`, `accent` and `danger` against `--ink` only (theme.js:249,251) — never against `--ink-2`/`--ink-3` — so a curated kit can ship with values that clear the floor against `--ink` but not against the elevated surfaces those same tokens are actually used on. Confirmed failures: Pink accentText/ink-3 = 4.014:1 (live on `.tool.today:hover`/`[aria-pressed]:hover`, styles.css:237, bg `--ink-3`); Harbor accentText/ink-3 = 4.397:1 (same); Harbor danger/ink-2 = 4.206:1 (live in the Share sheet's link warning `.lk-block .warn`, panels.css:245, and the "Delete this list everywhere" row `.menu button.danger`, panels.css:49 — both sit on `dialog.panel`'s ink-2 bg, panels.css:7-8, and `--danger` has no elevated "-2" companion the way `--dim`/`--muted` do, styles.css:405/panels.css:6); Teletype accent/ink-3 (focus ring) = 2.781:1, under the 3:1 UI-component floor. All under the DECISIONS.md "Theme engine" promise that these floors are guaranteed.
- proposed fix: in `finalize()`, fix `accentText`/`accent`/`danger` against `c.ink3` (matching what `derive()` already does), and give `--danger` an elevated `--danger-2` companion the same way `--dim`/`--muted` get `-2` variants.

### Keyboard focus is lost to the document body after Undo and after the save sheet closes
- severity: bug
- environment: desktop 1440×900, longtime fixture (+ a welcome→save flow for the second repro)
- steps: 1. Check a line off, wait for the "Done" toast with its Undo chip, click Undo. Read `document.activeElement` — it is `<body>`. 2. Separately: welcome → Skip → save sheet appears → click "I've saved it" (`#save-done`). Read `document.activeElement` — again `<body>`.
- evidence: `desktop-keyboard-focus.mjs`, `keyboard-focus.json` (`"focus after clicking Undo in the toast": {"landedOn":null}`, `"focus after closing the save sheet (save-done)": {"landedOn":null}` — `null` = body).
- why it matters: a keyboard-only user who undoes an action, or who just finished saving their link, has no idea where focus went; they must Tab from the top of the page to find their place again. Contrast with the same script's Escape-from-Settings case, which correctly and visibly returns focus to `#more`.
- proposed fix: after the toast closes and after the save sheet closes, move focus back to the row that was acted on (or its neighbour) and to the control that opened the save flow, respectively — the pattern DECISIONS.md already describes for keyboard-deleting a line.

### Touch hold-to-open-the-line-menu did not register in this test session (unconfirmed: harness artifact or real regression)
- severity: bug (unconfirmed — see caveats)
- environment: phone 390×844 (touch), longtime fixture
- steps: 1. `t.lineMenu("#list .row:first-child")` (a 650ms CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` hold then release on an undone row) times out waiting for `#p-line[open]`, both on a fresh isolated page and mid-sequence. 2. Raise the hold to 900ms and inspect `window.__tf()` immediately after release: `dragging:false, mark:"", panel:null` — no observable effect at all.
- evidence: `linemenu-fallback.mjs`, `linemenu-fallback.json`.
- why it matters: tapping `.tool.lmenu` does nothing by design on touch (`pointer-events:none` under `(hover:none)`, styles.css ~259, "stays for assistive tech only") — the only documented route to a line's menu on touch is hold-then-release (or swipe right). If that path is actually broken, touch users have no way to reach Edit/Repeat/Not today/Move/Delete for a line. But I could not confirm this is a real defect: `app.js`'s `longPressStart()` (~1454) gates on `e.pointerType === "touch"`, and the project's own `tools/e2e4.js` uses byte-for-byte the same `hold()` technique successfully as part of its release-gating suite. I could not determine whether CDP-synthesized touch events are failing to carry `pointerType:"touch"` in this harness/Chrome combination or whether something else in my session was the cause.
- proposed fix: not proposing a code change given the uncertainty; recommend running `node tools/e2e4.js` directly or testing on a real phone to confirm this path is healthy. I worked around it for target-size/naming purposes only via a direct DOM `.click()` on `.tool.lmenu`.

### A dragged, done line reads under 4.5:1 in six of twelve curated themes
- severity: papercut
- environment: pure Node (theme.js math) + desktop 1440×900 Everything view, longtime fixture, browser-confirmed
- steps: 1. In Everything, line font-size is `#all .row{font-size:clamp(1.1rem,1.9vh + .5vw,1.9rem)}` (styles.css:279) — 24.3px at 1440×900, as low as 17.6px on a short/narrow window. 2. `.row.dragging{background:var(--ink-3)}` (styles.css:323) while `.row.done{color:var(--done)}` (styles.css:152) — neither is scoped inside a `dialog.panel`, so the `--dim`/`--muted → -2` elevated-token swap doesn't apply, and there's no `--done-2`. 3. Confirmed live: apply Harbor, switch to Everything, add `.dragging` to a `.row.done` via `classList.add` (a style-state probe, not a full drag gesture) and read `getComputedStyle`.
- evidence: `contrast-fails.json` (done/ink-3: dark 3.378, light 3.611, pink 4.25, paper 4.152, harbor 3.875, cocoa 4.453), `contrast-browser-confirm.json` (Harbor 4.695→3.875, Light 4.561→3.611 at the real 24.3px computed size, exactly matching the Node prediction).
- why it matters: someone reordering a completed item sees its text noticeably dimmer while lifted, in 6 of 12 curated kits. It never drops under 3:1 in any case measured (the WCAG large-text floor holds), but fails the ordinary 4.5:1 body-text floor at Everything's smaller sizes.
- proposed fix: give `--done` the same elevated-background treatment `--dim`/`--muted` get, rather than leaving it to whatever floor it clears against `--ink` alone.

### The finale's glow "flare" still animates under prefers-reduced-motion
- severity: papercut
- environment: desktop 1440×900, longtime fixture, `reducedMotion: "reduce"`
- steps: open with `reducedMotion:"reduce"`, check off every remaining line to trigger the finale, read `document.getAnimations()` right after.
- evidence: `scaling-motion-sr.json` (`"finale + confetti under reducedMotion"` → `anims:[{"playState":"running","target":"glow"}]`), `motion-reduced-finale.png`.
- why it matters: `#glow.flare{opacity:1;transition:opacity .22s ease}` (styles.css:45) is more specific than the reduced-motion override `#glow{transition:none}` (styles.css ~448), so it wins; and the finale's trigger (`g.classList.add("flare")`, app.js:1057/1096) is never gated by `!RM.matches`, unlike almost every other animation trigger in app.js. Narrow leak — everything else checked (check-off kick/pop/arrive, sync-dot pulse, panel-close, drag, shuffle) correctly stops, and confetti is fully suppressed (`fx.js:37`,`fx.js:79`, both `if (get("reduced")) return;`, confirmed by zero canvas draw calls).
- proposed fix: add `#glow.flare{transition:none}` inside the existing reduced-motion media block, or gate the `classList.add("flare")` calls with `!RM.matches`.

### A checked-off line's remaining count is never announced to screen readers
- severity: papercut
- environment: desktop 1440×900, longtime fixture
- steps: install a MutationObserver on every live region/`role=status` in index.html (`#dot-sr`, `#toast`, `#mark-text`, `#review`, `#who-sr`), then check a line off.
- evidence: `scaling-motion-sr.json` (`"event":"check off a line"` → only `#toast`/`#toast .msg` fire, text "Done"; `#count`'s text is in no observed live region).
- why it matters: a screen-reader user hears "Done" but not "3 of 5" — the visible `<b>3</b>/5` updates silently. Doesn't block the task (they can navigate to `#count` and re-read it), but it's a real gap between what's announced and what a sighted user sees at a glance.
- proposed fix: append the count to the toast ("Done — 3 of 5"), or add a visually-hidden live region beside `#count`.

### Touch targets under 44×44 px on the phone environment
- severity: papercut
- environment: phone 390×844, longtime fixture, every surface (Today, Everything+search, ⋯ menu, line menu, section menu, Share, Settings + Day theme/Export/History/Removed/Templates, Lists + list detail, the `?` reference, welcome, save sheet)
- steps: for each surface, collect bounding boxes of every visible `button, a[href], input, select, textarea, [role=button], [role=tab], [role=checkbox], [tabindex]`, flag any under 44×44 CSS px.
- evidence: `surfaces-phone.mjs`, `surfaces-phone.json`, screenshots `size-*.png` (18 files, small targets outlined in red). Real offenders, deduplicated: `#count` (21×32 — notably narrow); `select#set-switch` (138.6×36); `select#set-pack` (167.6×36); `input#volume` (176×32); every sub-sheet's "‹ Back" button (48.4×32); `#set-addurl`/`#set-import-file` inputs (~110×42).
- why it matters: WCAG 2.5.8 wants ≥24×24 px with spacing (44×44 is the standard this app hits almost everywhere else on touch); all are still tappable, just tighter than the app's own norm. Note: `.tool.lmenu` measuring ~2×2 is **not** a defect — deliberately clipped with `pointer-events:none` under `(hover:none)` ("stays for assistive tech only"); touch users reach it via hold/swipe, so it isn't a "target" under 2.5.8. Excluded it and the intentionally off-screen 1×1 `#haptic` switch from this finding.
- proposed fix: give `select#set-switch`/`select#set-pack`/`input#volume`/the sub-sheet `.back` buttons `min-height:44px` the way `.chip`/`.add`/`#install .x` already get under `@media (hover:none)`; widen `#count`'s hit area.

### A few rough edges in Tab order
- severity: papercut
- environment: desktop 1440×900, longtime fixture
- steps: Tab repeatedly from a blank focus state on Today; open Settings and Tab repeatedly; click "Day theme"; click "‹ Back".
- evidence: `keyboard-focus.json` — one Tab press between "+ New line" and `#count` lands on nothing (`document.activeElement === body`), recovering on the very next Tab; inside Settings, Tab lands once on the `<dialog>` element itself between the last row and "× Close"; Back from Day theme lands focus on a generic `.body` container (no ring) rather than a specific control.
- why it matters: none of these lose or trap focus — the next Tab always recovers — but a keyboard user notices the empty stop, and the inconsistency between Escape (visibly returns to `#more`) and Back (lands on a ring-less container) on the same panel stack.
- proposed fix: low priority; have Back restore focus to the row that opened the sub-sheet, matching Escape's behavior.

## What this lens found solid

The theme engine's core promise holds up well under an adversarial sweep: all 20 randomly-seeded custom themes (10 dark, 10 light, seeded PRNG) passed every one of ~22 token-pair checks — text, muted, dim, done, accentText, danger, the checkbox hairline, and the accent-as-focus-ring, against ink/ink-2/ink-3 — with zero failures; every one of the 18 failures found lives in the nine non-original curated kits and traces to one specific, fixable asymmetry between `derive()` and `finalize()`. The engine's own "ink < ink-2 < ink-3 in relative luminance" assumption, which the whole ink-3-implies-ink/ink-2 shortcut depends on, held for all 34 themes tested with zero exceptions. Keyboard support is genuinely good: every one of 54 sampled Tab stops across Today, Everything and Settings showed a visible focus ring; modal panels correctly trap Tab (verified empirically); Escape from Settings visibly returns focus to the control that opened it; the "hover-only" ⋯ line-menu button on desktop is not a keyboard dead end — Tab reaches it, in order, right after its row's checkbox, confirmed live; and every shortcut key tested (A, O, /, T, Shift+T, M, ?, N, E, Cmd/Ctrl+Z, 1–9, -, Alt+↓, F) did what the `?` reference and README say it does. Reduced motion is implemented broadly and carefully — confetti is fully, deliberately suppressed (not just shortened), and check-off, drag, panel-close, and shuffle animations all correctly stand down — with only the one narrow glow-flare leak found above. Text scaling to 150%/200% (both as effective browser zoom and as a root font-size override on the phone viewport) produced no clipping, overlap, or lost content anywhere sampled; the rail grows in height gracefully rather than breaking. Names in the accessibility tree were consistently good everywhere sampled — the line menu's rows ("Edit", "Take off Today", "Repeat Never", "Not today · Back on Today tomorrow", "Move to another list…", "Delete"), the day/night toggle ("Switch to night"/"Switch to day"), and dialog names were all specific and accurate, with no dialog leaking its close button's label into its own name, matching what DECISIONS.md says was fixed.

What I could not check, and why: sync-status live-region behavior for a real offline/online transition — the local BroadcastChannel transport this audit requires doesn't appear to route through the network stack Playwright's `context.setOffline()` intercepts, so `#dot`'s state never left `"off"` in either direction even after a 3.5s wait each way; I cannot confirm or deny whether the offline/trouble/gone announcements actually fire on a real network. The touch hold-to-open-the-line-menu gesture (see that finding) is the one thing here I'd most want re-checked on a real phone or via `tools/e2e4.js` directly. I did not reach the iPad, Android, narrow, phoneLandscape, slow3g, or timezone-dependent environments, nor the day-review card or streak text, given the size of the surface list already in scope; I verified the theme-builder swatches' and the "‹ Back" button's focus rings by reading their CSS (`panels.css:90`, `panels.css:26`, both explicit `:focus-visible` rules) rather than by an empirical pass. And everything here approximates screen-reader behavior (MutationObserver on the same live regions an AT keys off) rather than verifying against a real VoiceOver/NVDA/JAWS session, which can differ in timing and interruption behavior from what the DOM alone predicts.
