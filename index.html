    Share

    Fair warning: anyone with the edit link can change anything, including making new links. Anyone with the view link can see everything until you rotate. No names, no record of who did what—"Who's here" only counts open devices.

    Save your link

    This link is the only way back to your list—and the only key that can read it. Nobody can send it to you again, not even the person running this site.

    • Put it in your password manager, same as a password.
    • On a phone, add this page to your Home Screen—the icon carries the link.
    • Open the new link on your other devices and add it to the Home Screen again. The old icon stops working.
    • Lose the link, lose the list.
