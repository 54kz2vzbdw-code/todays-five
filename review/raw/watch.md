### F1 · The face goes back to yesterday's count one minute after midnight — the exact failure the timeline exists to prevent · **bug**

- **where**: `apple/TodaysFive/[REDACTED]/ComplicationsProvider.swift:52-65` (`SnapshotTimeline.entries`): the `midnight > now` guard at :59-61 returns `[first]` built from the **unrolled** snapshot, and the reload policy at :64 is `.after(midnight + 60)`.
- **what was claimed**: `ComplicationsProvider.swift:5` "A face showing yesterday's count at nine in the morning is the failure this file exists to prevent" and :10 "the face is then right through the night with nothing running at all"; `PLAN-apple-phase3.md:244` "the count on the face is right through the night without anything running"; `DECISIONS-apple.md:742-743` the same sentence.
- **what I measured, with what**: `entries` copied verbatim into `tfprobe` (WidgetKit's two types stubbed), driven with a snapshot written at 22:00 (`done 3 / total 5`, `rollsAt` = the next midnight) and a disk that never changes because the app is not running — `review/raw/watch/probe.log`, section (g):
  ```
  getTimeline at 09-08 22:00:00: entries=[09-08 22:00:00→3/5, 09-09 00:00:00→0/2] reload at 09-09 00:01:00
  getTimeline at 09-09 00:01:00: entries=[09-09 00:01:00→3/5] reload at 09-09 01:01:00
  getTimeline at 09-09 01:01:00: entries=[09-09 01:01:00→3/5] reload at 09-09 02:01:00
  ```
  The midnight entry shows `0/2` for sixty seconds. The reload the policy itself asks for re-reads the same stale file, finds `rollsAt` in the past, takes the `midnight > now` branch and hands WidgetKit a single entry carrying `3/5` — and every hourly reload after that does the same. So with the app not running the face reads yesterday's count from 00:01 until the app next runs, which is nine in the morning for a watch left on a nightstand. - **evidence**: `review/raw/watch/probe.log`, `review/raw/watch/tools/tfprobe/Sources/tfprobe/main.swift` (section g quotes the source lines).
- **proposed repair (one concern)**: in the `midnight > now` guard, return the **rolled** entry: `return ([SnapshotEntry(date: now, snapshot: snapshot.afterRollover, showLine: showLine)], .after(now + 3600))`. `afterRollover` already zeroes `rollsAt`, so every later reload from the same stale disk lands on the same rolled numbers. ### F4 · A phone clock that was ever ahead poisons the channel for exactly the size of the error, and neither side can tell · **design** (admitted at `DECISIONS-apple.md:789-799`; quantified here)

- **where**: `WatchLinkSender.swift:70` `at = max(now, lastSentAt + 1)` — `lastSentAt` is an instance property, so monotonic per process only; `WatchLinkReceiver.swift:62,117,149` the persistent `tf/app/watch/appliedAt`; `WatchLink.swift:300` `guard let payload, payload.at > lastAppliedAt`. Nothing compares `at` to the receiver's own clock, and nothing ever lowers the mark.
- **what I measured, with what**: `WatchLinkReconciler.reconcile` itself, in `tfprobe` (`probe.log` section f):
  ```
  1. ahead payload: applied=true appliedAt-now=+3600000 ms
  2. honest removal 1 s later: applied=false remove=0  → the list stays on the wrist
  3. a new list 59 min 59 s later: applied=false
  4. the same, 1 h 0.001 s later: applied=true upsert=1  → outage = the clock error, to the millisecond
  ```
  During the outage *Remove from this device*, a new list, a nickname change and a Secret-kit lock/unlock are all silently dropped; the phone prints `sent` and the Watch prints `applied=false`, and both are telling the truth as they see it. A phone relaunch does not help (`lastSentAt` restarts at 0, `at` is `now` again).
- **what that instrument cannot see**: how often a phone clock is ahead by more than a few seconds in practice (NTP drift is seconds; a manual date change or a time-zone-confused clock is hours). Once is enough, since the mark is persistent.
- **evidence**: `review/raw/watch/probe.log`.
- **both sides**: sender — add a persisted, monotonic `seq` in `extra` (additive; `v` stays 1; an older Watch passes it through) and stamp `at` from it; receiver — treat a mark that is ahead of its own clock by more than a tolerance as evidence of a bad clock and accept a payload whose `at` is older than the mark but newer than "now − tolerance", or simply reset `appliedAt` when `appliedAt > now + 5 min`. Test for either: `reconcile(payload: at = now, lastAppliedAt: now + 3h, now: now)` must apply. Cost of the receiver-side rule alone: a payload replayed within the tolerance window applies twice, which the reconciler already makes harmless.

### F5 · `afterRollover`'s "total − done" is wrong by one for every recurring line, and by more than the comment admits · **papercut** (measured)

- **where**: `Shared/WatchSnapshot.swift:147-163`; the comment :153-155 "A recurring line that comes *back* at midnight is not modelled: the face under-counts by one for a morning rather than over-counting".
- **what rollover actually does** (`Rollover.swift`): step 1 (:59-109) files a finished line into History and then — if it has a live rule — **resets it in place** (`done=false`, `today=due`, :86-99) rather than tombstoning it (:100-106); step 2 (:111-120) puts a due recurring line that was off Today **onto** Today; step 3 (:122-138) brings a `return` back. All three leave Today larger than `total − done`.
- **what I measured, with what**: `Model.rollover` through the public API in `tfprobe` (`probe.log` section d), a five-line list with a home zone, two lines finished the previous afternoon:
  ```
  A plain:              afterRollover assumes 0/3  rollover gives 0/3  agree
  B daily rule:         afterRollover assumes 0/3  rollover gives 0/4  DISAGREE (face under-counts by 1)
  C due rule off Today: afterRollover assumes 0/4  rollover gives 0/5  DISAGREE (face under-counts by 1)
  D no zone, done 23:30: afterRollover assumes 0/4  rollover at 00:05 gives 1/5  at 09:00 gives 0/4
  ```
  So the direction in the comment is right (under-count), the magnitude is "one per recurring line or return due today", and the case the comment does not mention — a done recurring line resetting in place (B) — is the common one. D is the no-zone list (`COMPATIBILITY.md` §3's six-hour guard): the face says `0/4` at midnight, the app, if it runs at 00:05, publishes `1/5`, and nothing runs at 05:30 to file the line — so the face flips twice in one night. - **what that instrument cannot see**: the face. The counts are the core's own.
- **evidence**: `review/raw/watch/probe.log`.
- **proposed repair (one concern)**: the app has the core; let it compute the morning. Add `doneAfter`/`totalAfter` to the snapshot (additive, `v` stays 1, exactly as Phase 4 added `kit`/`pair`/`face`), computed as `Model.rollover(doc, today: <tomorrow in the home zone>, at: rollsAt).doc.todayItems` counts (for an edit link; for a view link, the unrolled counts, since the Watch will not roll it), and have `afterRollover` read them, falling back to `total − done` when absent. Test: a `TodayOpsTests` case with a daily rule asserting the morning counts equal `Model.rollover`'s.
